Who We Are

Built for Pune's Citizens

NagarSeva was launched under the Smart City Mission to digitize and streamline the grievance redressal process in Pune. Our platform ensures that every citizen's voice is heard and every issue is resolved within defined SLA timelines.

We believe in a city where no civic complaint goes unresolved — where government departments are accountable, and residents can trust their municipal corporation to deliver timely solutions.
